# Business and Financial Report — N-10 Wings

## 1. Revenue Model

Our platform generates revenue through two primary streams:

| Stream | Unit Price | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Organizer Fee** | Rs 5,000 | Charged to tournament organizers per event creation (5% of a standard Rs 100k pool). |
| **Player Commission** | Rs 10 | Charged per player registration (standardized to Rs 50 per team for 5-player rosters). |
